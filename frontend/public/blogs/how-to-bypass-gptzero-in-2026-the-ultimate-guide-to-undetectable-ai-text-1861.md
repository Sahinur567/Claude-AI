## How GPTZero Detects AI Text in 2026

To outsmart a detector, you must first understand how it evaluates text. While early versions of GPTZero relied almost entirely on basic measurements of randomness, modern iterations employ multi-layered analysis models that evaluate text across several key dimensions:

### 1. Perplexity (Unpredictability)
Perplexity measures how likely a word is to follow the previous one based on a language model's statistical training. AI generators are optimized for smoothness and logical probability; therefore, AI-generated text usually exhibits *low perplexity*. Human writing, by contrast, frequently uses unexpected phrasing, metaphors, and non-standard syntax, yielding *high perplexity*.

### 2. Burstiness (Structural Variation)
Burstiness refers to variations in sentence length, rhythm, and structure. AI models tend to generate uniform sentences with steady clauses and balanced lengths. Human authors naturally mix punchy, single-word sentences with long, sprawling compound structures. Low burstiness is one of the strongest flags for GPTZero.

### 3. Semantic Fingerprinting and Watermarking
In 2026, major AI developers and detection platforms rely on hidden statistical signatures—known as mathematical watermarks—embedded within generated output. GPTZero scans for these subtle token-frequency distributions and semantic clusters that human writers rarely replicate naturally.

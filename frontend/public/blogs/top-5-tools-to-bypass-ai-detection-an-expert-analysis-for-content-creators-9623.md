## How AI Detectors Spot Generated Text

Before diving into the top platforms, it is essential to understand what these tools are modifying. AI detectors primarily measure two linguistic metrics:

1. **Perplexity:** A measure of how unpredictable or complex the text is. Human writing tends to feature surprising word choices and unique phrasing, resulting in high perplexity. AI writing, by contrast, selects statistically probable words, keeping perplexity low.
2. **Burstiness:** The variation in sentence structure and length. Humans naturally mix short, punchy sentences with longer, intricate compound thoughts. AI models usually produce sentences of uniform length and rhythm.

Advanced detection engines also look for embedded digital watermarks—subtle mathematical signatures left behind by LLMs. To pass these checks, effective AI converters must re-structure content at a foundational level without losing the core message.

## Best Practices: Combining Humanizers with Manual Refinement

While leveraging the **best AI humanizers** saves hours of editing, automated software should complement—not replace—a thorough editorial review. To guarantee high engagement and natural flow, follow these editorial tips:

* **Inject Personal Experience:** Add real-world anecdotes, unique case studies, or proprietary data that AI models cannot guess or replicate.
* **Vary Formatting and Pacing:** Break up dense paragraphs with bullet points, subheadings, rhetorical questions, and varied sentence structures.
* **Eliminate AI Clichés:** Watch out for repetitive transitional phrases like "In conclusion," "Furthermore," and "It is important to remember."
